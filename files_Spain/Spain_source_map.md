## Source classes used

- Authoritative cultural/institutional: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (10 cards), RAE historical dictionary, Turespaña and regional tourism authorities (Asturias, Euskadi, Galicia, Extremadura, Comunitat Valenciana).
- Official data/government: INE, Ministry of Education/Sports (CSD), Ministry of Culture and Agriculture via La Moncloa, Seguridad Social, Eurydice, Ministry of Rights (large families), administracion.gob.es, regional statistics offices (Eustat, IAEST Aragón), regional education departments.
- Regional/local and reference: Canarian and Cantabrian sport bodies and public broadcaster, city tourism offices, university documents, and press (medium quality).

## Sources investigated but not used

Commercial festival/travel sites (festivalesdeespana.com, Time Out, Movelia, Carglass and similar blogs), forum pages, political motions on Catalan schooling, sources from other countries returned by mistake (Chile, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia time-use surveys), Wikipedia (not used as evidence), and sources not opened: Instituto Cervantes, Biblioteca Nacional de España, Ministry of Culture intangible-heritage inventory, Idescat, IAPH, INE time-use survey.

## Gaps in the source ecosystem

No Basque-language or Galician-language institutional pages were mined beyond one Galician educational resource; Catalan sources are Fem Turisme, betevé, XTEC and UPF documents. Family-gathering traditions and school traditions had few citable institutional pages.
