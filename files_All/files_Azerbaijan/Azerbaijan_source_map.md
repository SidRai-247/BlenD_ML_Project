# Azerbaijan KB — Source Map

## Scope
Six BLEnD categories, 25 evidence cards each; 150 cards total. Sources were researched before extraction. The corpus is evidence-backed and region-aware, not an exhaustive representation of Azerbaijani culture.

## Core source domains
- UNESCO / UNESCO National Commission: intangible heritage, foodways, celebrations, music, sport, crafts.
- Azerbaijan Travel: regional food, tea, silk, leisure and locality-specific practices.
- State Statistical Committee: education, culture, sport, family/gender and labour-market statistics.
- Ministry of Science and Education: school language, curriculum, olympiads and school sport.
- State Committee for Family, Women and Children Affairs: family policy, parenting and support services.
- Permanent Delegation of Azerbaijan to UNESCO: public holidays.

## Source-quality rule
Official government, UNESCO and national cultural-institution sources are marked high; tourism-board pages are marked medium when used for regional food/tourism-specific practices.

## Geographic coverage
Regional evidence includes Lankaran, Shaki, Lahij and Nakhchivan, plus national-level evidence. Official statistical sources provide economic-region coverage. This is structured regional coverage, not exhaustive geographic representation.
